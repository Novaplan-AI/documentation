---

Novaplan AI supports several sign-in methods. The methods available to you depend on your organization's workspace configuration. Novaplan prepares most settings and invites users to join; administrators should coordinate changes to sign-in with the Novaplan team and, where applicable, their identity-provider administrator.

## Where administrators manage sign-in

An authorized workspace administrator can open **Workspace Settings → Authentication** to review the enabled methods and their configuration. Other users should follow the sign-in method shown for their workspace. If you received an invitation, start with [Getting started](/quickstart).

<Warning>
  Changing an active sign-in method can prevent users from accessing the workspace. For invitation-based access, confirm that automatic account creation (JIT) is disabled on any new identity-provider method. Agree on test accounts for both invited and uninvited users and an administrator recovery path with Novaplan AI before changing production authentication.
</Warning>

## Available methods

| Method | What it does | Setup guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Password | Sign in with an email address and password. | [Password](/auth/password) |
| One-time password (OTP) | Sign in with a code delivered by email. Workspace email delivery must be configured. | [OTP](/auth/otp) and [SMTP](/smtp) |
| Google | Sign in with a Google account through OAuth 2.0. | [Google](/auth/google) |
| Microsoft | Sign in with a Microsoft account through OAuth 2.0. | [Microsoft / Azure AD](/auth/microsoft-azureAd) |
| Other OAuth/OIDC provider | Connect a supported identity provider using its authorization, token, and user-info endpoints. | [OAuth/OIDC](/auth/oauth) |
| SAML SSO | Use your organization's SAML identity provider for single sign-on. | [SAML](/auth/saml) |

An identity-provider integration requires the redirect URI and other values shown in your workspace. Use the exact values from the deployed Novaplan AI configuration rather than copying an address from a screenshot.

## If sign-in fails

1. Confirm that you are using the email address that received the invitation and the method configured for your workspace.
2. If you use OTP or password reset, check email delivery and your spam folder.
3. If you use an identity provider, ask your administrator to check its app assignment, redirect URI, and any relevant certificate or consent settings.
4. Ask an authorized administrator to review the available workspace error details. Do not send passwords, tokens, or one-time codes in support messages.

If you cannot regain access, contact your workspace administrator or [Novaplan AI support](mailto:support@novaplan.ai).
