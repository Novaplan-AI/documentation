## Contact Novaplan

To discuss a new workspace or request a demo, use our [contact form](https://www.novaplan.ai/contact).

For help with an existing workspace, email [support@novaplan.ai](mailto:support@novaplan.ai).

## Workspace access and setup

The Novaplan team creates your organization's workspace and invites you to join it. If you need access, cannot find your invitation, or have questions about setup, contact us at the address above.

If you already have an invitation, see [Getting started](/quickstart).

## Looking for an installation guide?

Previous deployment and installation guide links now lead here. Novaplan AI is a closed-source product, and its customer documentation does not provide self-installation instructions. Use our [contact form](https://www.novaplan.ai/contact) to discuss your workspace requirements with the Novaplan team.

## What to include in your message

- Your organization or workspace name.
- A short description of what you were trying to do.
- Any error message you saw, with sensitive information removed.

Do not include passwords, API keys, access tokens, or invitation links.
