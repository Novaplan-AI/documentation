The Novaplan team creates your organization's workspace and invites you to join it. Start with the invitation sent to your email address.

<div id="requirements" />

## Before you begin

- Have your Novaplan AI invitation email ready.
- Use the email address that received the invitation.
- Follow the workspace link in that email.

<div id="setup" />

## Join your workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your invitation">
    Open the invitation email and follow its link to your workspace.

    If you cannot find the email, check your spam folder. If it is still missing, contact the person who arranged your access or [Novaplan AI support](mailto:support@novaplan.ai).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign in">
    Follow the instructions on the sign-in screen. The available sign-in methods depend on your organization's workspace settings.

    Use the invited email address, including when signing in through your organization's identity provider.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check your workspace access">
    Once signed in, check that you are in the workspace you were invited to join.

    If your workspace is missing or you see an access error, contact your workspace administrator or [Novaplan AI support](mailto:support@novaplan.ai).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start using Novaplan AI">
    Ask a question about content available in your workspace. Review the sources provided with the answer to check the underlying information.

    The content you can find depends on what has been added or connected and the permissions assigned to you. Ask your workspace administrator if expected content is missing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Invitation and sign-in help

| Situation | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Your invitation has expired | Ask your workspace administrator or Novaplan AI support to send a new invitation. |
| The invitation was sent to the wrong email address | Ask the person who invited you to correct the address and send a new invitation. |
| You cannot use the sign-in method shown | Contact your workspace administrator to confirm which method your organization uses. |
| You reach an unexpected setup screen | Contact your workspace administrator or Novaplan AI support before changing workspace settings. |

## For workspace administrators

To invite colleagues and manage their access, see [Users](/user-management/user). Only administrators with the appropriate permissions should change workspace settings.

For help getting started, email [support@novaplan.ai](mailto:support@novaplan.ai).
