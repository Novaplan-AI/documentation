## Workspace access

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I get access to Novaplan AI?">
    The Novaplan team creates your organization's workspace and invites you by email. Follow the invitation and sign in with the email address it was sent to. See [Getting started](/quickstart).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if my invitation is missing or has expired?">
    Check your spam folder and confirm which email address was invited. Contact the person who arranged your access or [Novaplan support](/contact-us) if you need a new invitation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to install or deploy Novaplan AI?">
    No. Use the workspace provided by Novaplan. These customer guides do not require you to install the platform or manage servers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who configures the workspace?">
    Novaplan manages most workspace setup and administration. The configuration guides remain available as a reference. Your team may need to approve access to its connected applications. Only change administrator settings that are part of your agreed responsibilities; see the [administrator setup reference](/onboarding).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who can help with a workspace problem?">
    Email [support@novaplan.ai](mailto:support@novaplan.ai). Include your organization, a description of the issue, and any error message, with sensitive information removed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## AI Models

If AI model settings are part of your agreed setup responsibilities, see the [AI Providers reference](/ai-models/overview). Contact [Novaplan support](/contact-us) to confirm which settings apply to your workspace before changing providers or credentials.
